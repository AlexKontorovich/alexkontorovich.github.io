Lecture 1: Basic Definitions

In this course, we'll prove fundamental theorems in Euclidean geometry using the Lean 4 proof assistant.

Definition: Point and Line

We begin with the undefined concepts of point and line, related by the incidence relation.

Notation: \(P \in \ell\) means point \(P\) lies on line \(\ell\).

Axiom 1: Incidence

For any two distinct points, there exists exactly one line containing both points.
