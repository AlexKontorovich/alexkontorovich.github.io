Euclidean Geometry with Lean 4

A formal approach to classical geometry
Spring 2025

Instructor Information

Professor: Alex Kontorovich

Institution: Rutgers University

Course Description: This course explores classical Euclidean geometry through a modern lens, using the Lean 4 proof assistant to formalize definitions, theorems, and proofs. Students will gain deep understanding of geometric reasoning while learning contemporary approaches to mathematical formalization.
